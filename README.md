## A little about me

I like turning ideas from different fields into tools that can be tested, understood, and used. My projects move between **scientific computing**, **music technology**, and **systems engineering**—with a recurring interest in making complex behaviour explicit rather than hiding it behind a black box.

I'm also interested in AI, philosophy, and the study of cognition. Outside the editor, music and instruments are a constant source of curiosity.

## How I like to build

- **Make assumptions visible.** Inputs, units, provenance, and limitations should be inspectable.
- **Prefer useful tools over demos.** A project should solve a concrete problem and be usable outside its source code.
- **Respect the system boundary.** Hardware, operating systems, browsers, and scientific models all have constraints worth documenting honestly.
